## 3. The dispatch's specific question: would enforcing required turn angle break the degeneracy? NO.

This was `#680`'s own honestly-flagged gap ("V-infinity-magnitude-only ⇒ necessary-not-
sufficient for true periodicity (no required-turn check)"). Closing the gap does not isolate
asymmetric roots — it kills them:

- **Turn-angle checks are inequalities, not equalities.** In the patched-conic ballistic-flyby
  model the bend at each flyby is a free design parameter (periapsis-radius choice), bounded by
  achievability: `0 ≤ δ_required ≤ δ_max(|v∞|, r_p,min)`. Inequalities cannot reduce the
  solution set's dimension — they select **sub-arcs** of the same 1-manifold. Verified
  (check C): along the `n_rev=(0,0)` line the required turn at the flyby moon varies smoothly
  (137° → 156° over β 305→359), as does the moon-local v∞ direction mismatch at the anchor.
  (Those required turns are incidentally far beyond any achievable bend at these small moons —
  the asymmetric arc is also physically dead on achievability, independent of the degeneracy
  argument.)
- **The one missing EQUALITY collapses onto the symmetric family.** True periodicity of the
  A-B-A cycle additionally requires the pattern-repeat (resonance) condition
  `(n_a − n_b)·2·tof ≡ 0 (mod 360°)` — without it there is no "required turn at the anchor" to
  enforce at all, because the next cycle's geometry differs and the same legs do not recur. On
  the manifold `β ≡ (n_a−n_b)·tof (mod 180°)`, the repeat condition reads `2β ≡ 0 (mod 360°)`,
  i.e. **β ∈ {0°, 180°} — exactly the symmetric slice**, i.e. the already-catalogued
  `#563`/`#569` family. The per-cycle pattern drift along the asymmetric arc is `2β (mod 360°)`
  (up to sign) — e.g. 280° at β=320° — blatantly non-repeating (check D; the β≡0 identity is
  three lines of algebra, not numerics).
- So the strictest formulation does not turn the continuum into a discrete set of new
  asymmetric closures; it **proves genuinely-asymmetric true-periodic closures do not exist in
  this equal-leg-time genome at all**. (Repeat-after-m-cycles variants force
  `β ≡ 0 (mod 180°/m)` but the intermediate cycles then need *different* legs — that is a

All of this is conditional on the idealized circular-coplanar patched-conic model — the same
