## 0. Verdict

This is the short note that named the "Venus swingby mode": one Venus flyby on the return (or outbound)
leg of a one-shot Mars stopover or flyby mission, to cut Earth entry speed. Nothing is periodic or
repeated. There is no literal collision for `#942` R1(c).

## 1. Content (READ)

- **Claim (p.565):** Venus swingby cuts Earth entry speed from up to 70,000 fps to 50,000 fps or less
  "without increases in mission propulsion requirements". 14 opportunities, 1971-1999, were examined.
- **Cycle (p.566):** Mars opportunities come about every 26 months. Because of the Mars eccentricity,
  "the cyclic pattern of mission profile variations repeats every 15 yr, or every seven oppositions".
  - Check: 7 x 779.9 d = 5459 d = 14.9 yr.
- **Direct baseline (p.566):** 10-day stopover, 500-km Mars parking orbit by aerobraking, 4-6-month
  outbound and 6-8-month return legs. Entry speed ranges from 46,000 fps (1971) to 70,000 fps (1980).
- **1975 example (p.567):**
  - The direct return crosses inside the Venus orbit and meets Earth at a 31 deg relative path angle,
    which gives 65,600 fps entry.
  - With a dark-side Venus passage at about 3300 km altitude, the heliocentric speed drops by 15,000 fps.
    The Earth approach is at 14 deg and entry is 44,000 fps.
  - Gross weight: 1.43 x 10^6 lb. Biasing the direct trajectory to 60,000 fps gives 1.70 x 10^6 lb;
    retro-braking to 60,000 fps gives 1.61 x 10^6 lb; retro-braking to 50,000 fps gives 2.05 x 10^6 lb.
- **Coverage (p.567):**
  - About two-thirds of the opportunities can use a homebound Venus swingby, sometimes after a short wait
    at Mars.
  - The rest can use an outbound swingby: a long (>180 deg) transfer out and a short (<180 deg) transfer
    back, made affordable by the Venus "whip effect".
  - 1980 outbound swingby: total propulsion 27,600 fps (1971: 27,500), entry 41,500 fps, 495 days,
    +3.5% gross weight.
  - Trips with a Venus passage before perihelion stretch to about 500 days.
- **Flyby missions (p.567):** stopover missions convert to Mars flyby missions with powered Mars turns of
  1600-5000 fps.

## 2. Gate relevance

- **`#942` R1(c):** no collision (one-shot, not periodic). It is history for a Venus-Mars result: the
  origin of the swingby mode. Gillespie & Ross 1967 (filed today) gives the type classification and the
  E-V-M commensurability.
- The 15-yr / 7-opposition cycle is the E-M eccentricity cycle, not an E-V-M one. Do not confuse it with
  the Gillespie-Ross 6.4-yr syzygistic period.
