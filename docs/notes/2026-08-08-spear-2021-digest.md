## What this thesis contains

Computes 18 periodic-orbit families in the planar Neptune-Triton CR3BP, including **twelve resonant
families**: 1:2, 1:3, 2:1, 2:3, 3:1, 3:2 — TWO distinct families per ratio (labeled "family 1"/
"family 2" throughout, Ch. 4.3, Figs. 4.13-4.36), each with in-plane and out-of-plane stability
analysis. **No 4:5 or 4:7 family appears anywhere in this thesis** — confirmed by direct text search
(the only resonance ratios present are 1:2/1:3/2:1/2:3/3:1/3:2). `#781`'s own claimed object (the
4:5-saddle resonant orbit and its homoclinic self-connection) is genuinely absent from this work.

The thesis's own actual contribution: selects TWO of its own periodic-orbit families (L1/L2 Lyapunov
orbits, not the resonant families) and constructs **planar HETEROCLINIC connections** between them
via Poincaré maps, invariant-manifold intersections, multiple-shooting correction, and continuation
— 11 exact connections at three energy levels. This is a genuine, rigorous prior-art precedent for
manifold-connection construction in this specific system, but for a DIFFERENT connection type
(heteroclinic between two distinct orbit families, not homoclinic self-connection of one resonant
orbit) and DIFFERENT orbit families (L1/L2 Lyapunov, not resonant).

**Zero homoclinic content**: the thesis's own occurrences of "homoclinic" are literature-review/
citation context only, not its own constructions — confirmed by direct text search, corroborating
the adversarial review's own finding.

**Future-work framing** (per the adversarial review, not independently re-verified word-for-word in
this digest pass): explicitly lists resonant-orbit connection design (naming 1:2, 2:3, 4:3) as future
work, i.e. NOT YET DONE even for the resonant families it did compute — further supporting that
`#781`'s own resonant-orbit homoclinic connection is genuinely beyond this thesis's own scope, not a
quiet reproduction of it.
