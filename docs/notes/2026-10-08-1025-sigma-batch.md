## 2. Model and corrector (the #1004/#1034 method, generalised to N nodes)

- Model: R-S 2009 circular coplanar Jovian system (`run_942_enumerate.cell_system("gc")`), point-mass
  Jupiter (`MU_JUPITER_KM3_S2`), Ganymede and Callisto both massive. Joint sigma: both moons' GMs and
  softening radii are sigma times the real values (`SATELLITES`).
- Unknowns: per node the moon-relative position and velocity offsets and the epoch (7 per node).
- Rows: forward-backward match at each leg's mid-time (velocity rows weighted 1e3), periapsis gauge at
  each node (weight 1e4); the last leg closes on the first node rotated by Ganymede's advance over
  T = k S_GC (rigid in the circular model). Square system. DOP853 + analytic STM, rtol 1e-13,
  atol 1e-12.
- Solve: damped Newton (column-scaled least squares, step capped at half the node offset,
  backtracking), then trf with the analytic Jacobian if Newton stalls.
- Floors: 1e-3 km, 1e-6 km/s, gauge 1e-9. Noise-floor rule (#968): intermediate points (sigma < 1)
  are accepted at 1e-2 km and 1e-6 km/s. The sigma = 1 end point needs the floors.

Deviations from #1004/#1034, all recorded here before the runs:
1. Start at sigma = 0.02 (not 0.05), so the identity steps sit at sigma <= 0.01 as the lead asked.
2. rtol / atol 1e-13 / 1e-12 (#1004 used 1e-12 / 1e-10; #968 tightened them for the noise floor).
   Cost measured: 0.6-0.7 s per residual-plus-Jacobian at sigma = 0.1 (gc-2, gc6-2).
3. Seed periapses from `periapsis_node(..., max_offset_km=1e12)` (unclamped; the 0.6 SOI clamp gave
   wrong seed turns in #1043/#1044). Offsets scaled by sigma.
4. Moon-relative node coordinates (better conditioned at small sigma, #968 rung (b)). The physics is
   unchanged.
5. Natural step: secant predictor in log sigma, factor 1.2, halved on failure, regrown x2 in
   (factor - 1) after a success, capped at 1.2; stop when the step is below 1e-5.

## 3. Per-member procedure and fixed criteria

1. Converge at sigma = 0.02 from the scaled seed. If it fails: NUMERICAL STOP.
2. Identity by the limit (#1034 amendment 1): converge at sigma = 0.01, 0.005, 0.0025 (each from the
   previous, offsets scaled). PASS if a point at sigma <= 0.005 converges with the maximum node
   |V_inf - V_inf(patched conic)| < 0.005 km/s and the shift does not grow as sigma falls (tolerance
   1e-4 km/s). If it fails: IDENTITY FAIL (the run does not go up; no claim on another branch).
3. Natural continuation up to sigma = 1 (sec. 2 item 5).
4. Impact: a converged point with a periapsis at or inside sigma x R (`SATELLITES` radius) ends the
   member as IMPACT at that sigma (#1034 sec. 4). The project floors (Ganymede 100 km, Callisto 200 km,
   scaled by sigma) are recorded per point as `floor_ok` and in the summary; they are a flag, not an
   end.
5. If the natural step falls below 1e-5 at sigma_f: monitor-variable continuation (#1034 sec. 2:
   sigma an unknown, the state component that changed most over the last secant, column-scaled, is
   stepped and fixed by an extra row; no SVD tangent is ever used, which enforces the tangent rule
   trivially). The monitor step grows x1.5 after a convergence in at most 4 iterations (cap x4 of the
   secant), halves on failure, at most six halvings.
   - If the monitor sigma rises above sigma_f + 1e-4: not a turning point. The point joins the branch,
     the stop is recorded in `passed_stops`, natural continuation resumes (factor 1.01).
   - If the monitor sigma falls below 0.91 sigma_f: the fold checks (item 6).
   - Six halvings without convergence: NUMERICAL STOP.
6. Fold checks (#1004 amendment 1, generalised): two solutions at fixed sigma = 0.915 sigma_f and
   0.976 sigma_f (gc-2: 0.150 and 0.160), Newton from the nearest lower-branch and upper-branch
   points. Supported at a sigma if both converge to the floors and some node differs by more than
   0.02 km/s in V_inf and 100 km in r_p / sigma. FOLDS if supported at both sigmas; otherwise
   NUMERICAL STOP. Also recorded (#1034 sec. 3 check 3): direct Newton at sigma = 1 from the seed and
   from both branches' last points. A converged orbit there is recorded, not identified with the member.
7. At sigma = 1: IAS15 re-fly (REBOUND, epsilon 1e-11, the same scaled GMs and softening radii; #1004
   verify check 3) of every half-arc against DOP853. PASS: < 1e-2 km and < 1e-7 km/s. EXISTS is
   reported with the IAS15 result; an IAS15 fail is reported as such, not hidden.

Outcomes: EXISTS (with IAS15 pass/fail) / FOLDS at sigma_f / IMPACT at sigma_i / IDENTITY FAIL /
NUMERICAL STOP (reason). Only EXISTS with an IAS15 pass means "exists in the continuous ideal model".

## 8. AMENDMENT 1 (robustness fix, 2026-10-08 17:27 AEDT; criteria unchanged)

The #1033 run (which reuses this driver) found that `monitor_step` crashes with a LinAlgError when the
sigma-derivative probe hits a failed propagation: the residual is inf, so the difference is NaN. The
fix treats a non-finite Jacobian or residual, or a failed lstsq, as a failed monitor attempt (the
step is halved, as for any failed attempt). The batch workers launched before this fix run the old
code. A worker that hits the case exits with a traceback, and its later members do not run. Relaunching the same command resumes
every member from its checkpoint with the fix.

## 9. AMENDMENT 2 (registered before the batch analysis; from #1033)

#1033 found a converged sigma = 1 orbit that passes 83 km from Ganymede's centre between two flyby
nodes. That is through the softened interior, so it is not a physical orbit. The driver checks impact
only at the nodes. So every member reported EXISTS gets a post-hoc unscheduled-pass scan at sigma = 1
(DOP853, rtol 1e-12, 0.0005-day sampling, every leg, both moons). A closest approach inside sigma x R
turns EXISTS into IMPACT (unscheduled). A pass inside one Hill radius is recorded, as in #1034. The
same scan runs on the last accepted point of every FOLDS member, to check that the branch was
physical up to the fold.
