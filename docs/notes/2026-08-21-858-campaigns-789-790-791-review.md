## 1. Executive summary and ranking

None of the three should be greenlit **as written**. All three are honest about their own biggest
risks, but all three are census-shaped scale-ups registered before the one structural problem their
own arc exposed — **zero catalogue rows from `#753`-`#786` despite real findings** — was fixed, and
two of the three carry cost estimates contradicted by the project's own measured history.

Ranked by expected value:

1. **#789 (Resonant Atlas)** — greenlight a **narrowed pilot** (3 systems + 1 positive-control
   system, family-stage-first triage, ~2-3 days wall), NOT the 15-20-system / 2-4-week sweep. It is
   the only campaign of the three whose method has a demonstrated novel-finding mechanism (1 novel
   hit in 4 systems, `#781`). Gate the full census on (a) the pilot finding anything in-band on a
   new system and (b) a writeback path actually existing (see §2 — today it does not, even under
   `#838`'s just-delivered design).
2. **#790 (itinerary enumeration)** — **defer**, with a cheap symmetric-only mini-pilot as the
   unblocking probe. Its cost model ("minutes-to-an-hour per closure") is contradicted by the one
   real data point: the `#768`→`#773`→`#775`→`#782`→`#774` saga took FIVE dispatches over ~8 days
   to close ONE 2-symbol chain, and succeeded only via a 1-DOF symmetric corrector that does not
   generalize to asymmetric itineraries. The general case is blocked by a real, named capability
   gap (no Jacobi-constrained multiple-shooting corrector). But #790 — not #789 — is where the
   catalogue-shaped prize lives (`#752`: ballistic resonance-transition planet-moon cyclers), so
   the deferral is "until the pilot probe reports", not "indefinitely".
3. **#791 (moon-tour sequence enumeration)** — **fund only Stage 0/1** (Tisserand pruning + the
   patched-conic enumeration + per-sequence empty-region stamps), with an explicit stop-and-report
   checkpoint BEFORE any n-body shooting. The n-body half should additionally wait for `#849`'s
   reposing fix, since the `#388` wall evidence it would be interpreted against is partly
   contaminated. Expected outcome is a large, legitimate empty-region census — a registry
   deliverable, not a discovery one — and the review below argues its per-sequence sampling density
   is too sparse to be a fair test of anything else.

If the owner can fund exactly one thing this week: the **#789 pilot** (§6).

## 2. The shared structural problem: there is still nowhere to put the output

This is the single most load-bearing fact for all three proposals, and `#789`'s own registration
half-admits it ("needs a writeback design up front, or weeks of CPU pile up uncatalogued in
`data/found/`"). The evidence:

- The entire `#753`-`#786` arc — four systems, ~20 dispatches, one genuinely novel result —
  produced **zero catalogue rows**. `#781`'s Neptune-Triton homoclinics (the arc's one novel hit,
  "first resonant-orbit manifold connection of any kind in the Neptune-Triton CR3BP") are still
  sitting in a results note, explicitly "NOT yet written back" per its own bullet.
- The two existing writeback designs do not cover this output class. `#770` concluded a homoclinic
  self-connection is **supplementary evidence on an existing row, not a row** ("a homoclinic
  SELF-connection has no laps"). `#838` (delivered 2026-08-21, awaiting approval) proposes a
  dedicated `data/manifold_connections.yaml` registry — but its admission rule requires **at least
  one endpoint to resolve to a catalogue row id**. Neptune-Triton has zero catalogue rows, so
  `#781`'s finding is inadmissible under `#838` as designed. Every fresh system `#789` opens has
  the same property.
- Therefore, scaling `#789` 15-20× today manufactures 15-20 copies of the `#781` situation:
  verified findings with no structural home. That is not a hypothetical — it is the arc's actual,
  already-observed terminal state.

**Concrete fix, cheap, and it is a prerequisite not a parallel task**: before ANY multi-system
dispatch, (a) settle `#838` (approve/implement, and either extend its admission rule for the
zero-row-system case or decide the connection registry admits inline-both-endpoints entries with
mandatory identity evidence), and (b) **write back `#781` through the settled path as the
end-to-end test of it**. If the project cannot catalogue the one known-good finding it already
has, a 15-system atlas is premature by construction. `#857` (website representation) is the
natural companion and is already registered.

The second shared observation is the value inversion between #789 and #790. Per `#752`'s own
scoping verdict, the catalogue-eligible object this whole program unlocks is the **periodic
resonance-transition chain orbit** (Vaquero Fig-4.10-class, `#782`'s converged object — repeated
moon encounters, genuinely cycler-shaped), not the homoclinic connection, which is scaffolding.
So #789 standalone produces scaffolding; #790 produces the prize; and #789's right size is
therefore "what #790's pilot needs as feedstock", not "everything the registry can instantiate."

## 3. Campaign #789 — "the Resonant Atlas"

### 3.1 What the track record actually says

Per-system outcomes of the method being scaled (all read from the tasks' own bullets/notes, not
the proposal's framing):

| System | Family gate | Connection stage | Catalogue rows |
|---|---|---|---|
| Jupiter-Europa (`#753`-`#759`) | mixed (1/4 → later 5:6-LO confirmed by review) | honest close-FAIL on Table 2 (x off ~11× tol); Table-3 heteroclinic blocked by λ≈4445 wall | 0 |
| Saturn-Titan (`#765`-`#767`) | 2/4 (one printed-period source error flagged) | POSITIVE — 4 self-consistency homoclinics, ghost margins 148-307× | 0 |
| Neptune-Triton (`#776`/`#777`/`#781`) | clean 10/10 | **NOVEL** — 2 homoclinics, first in this system; 4:7 honest FAIL at λ≈1.46e4 | 0 |
| Earth-Moon (`#780`/`#783`/`#786`) | 12/16 | two independent clean negatives (BMO conditioning wall; dense-crossing basin starvation) | 0 |

So the real hit rate for a *novel* connection is **1 in 4 systems**, the positive-anything rate is
2 in 4, and the phenomenon itself is published prior art in 3 of the 4 (Anderson-Lo, Vaquero incl.
her Earth-Moon Sec. 4.4 found by `#786`'s own gate). "Direct scale-up of the ONLY method that
produced a novel finding this session" is literally true and still consistent with a 15-system
sweep yielding ~3-4 novel-connection systems — plausibly the best EV per CPU-hour on the current
menu, IF the writeback gap is closed and IF the viable-system count is real (it isn't — §3.2).

### 3.2 The "~15-20 viable systems" estimate is optimistic; the tractable band is measurable first

Two independent narrowing effects the proposal does not price in:

- **Eigenvalue tractability band.** Every converged connection this arc has produced sits at
  |λ| ≈ 55 (`#766`) to ≈ 2130 (`#767`); every attempt beyond ~4×10³ failed honestly (`#759`
  λ≈4445 "fractally sensitive"; `#781`'s 4:7 at 1.46e4, Newton stalled at 1.7e-3-3.4e-3), and
  near-unit eigenvalues are infeasible in the other direction (5:6-LI λ=1.000008, manifold
  e-folding ~4.7e6 tu — the paper itself discarded it). Whether a (system, p:q, C) cell lands
  in-band is knowable from the **family stage alone**, which is cheap (the corrector already
  returns the monodromy; `#777`'s heaviest single family-continuation test runs 376 s
  single-threaded, and family gates for whole systems landed in single sessions).
- **Registry reality.** `core/satellites.py` can indeed instantiate ~40 pairs with zero new code,
  but the mass-ratio spread is brutal: the Europa-like band (µ ~ 1e-5-1e-4: Io, Europa, Ganymede,
  Callisto, Titania, Oberon, Ariel, Umbriel) has ~8 members net of the 4 already done; the next
  tier (Rhea/Dione/Iapetus/Enceladus, µ ~ 2e-6-4e-6) is an order of magnitude lighter and
  untested for this method; the rest (Amalthea, Proteus, Mimas, the asteroid moons) are µ ≤ 1e-7
  where `#607`/`#599`-class mass-limited negatives are already on file for related machinery.
  Also Pluto-Charon is structurally excluded (µ≈0.109 breaks the two-body seed construction,
  `#764`) and Uranus-Oberon connections are **published** (AAS 24-288, per `#752`) — which is
  actually useful, see §3.4.

Realistic novel-viable count: **~8-10, not 15-20**, before the family-stage survey prunes further.

### 3.3 Cost sanity — the estimate is the right order, and that is not the problem

Measured unit costs from the arc's own notes: `correct_connection` 12-40 s/call (up to ~70 s on
hard targets) (`#781` note); the direct manifold-tube pre-scan that seeds it, 81 s for a full
branch×k scan (`#781`), ~22 s in `#786` "vs. tens of minutes" for the blind Newton grid. At the
proposal's own 30-40k connection attempts that is ~150-800 CPU-hours, plus a family stage in the
tens of CPU-hours — the stated 1-3k CPU-hours is the right order (slightly high). The problem is
not the total; it is that ~90% of it is spent on the energy axis (~100-step continuation × ~40
ratios × N systems) **before** knowing which cells are in-band. Inverting the order (survey first,
connections only in-band) plausibly cuts the connection stage by a large factor for free.

### 3.4 Recommended shape

**Greenlight a pilot, not the census.** Pilot = 3 new systems + 1 positive control:

- **Uranus-Oberon as the positive control** (published connections, AAS 24-288): per
  `[[feedback_verify_gauntlet_with_positive_control]]`, an unanchored-system evidence pattern
  (`#765`/`#768`-style, as the proposal itself proposes) must first be shown to fire on a system
  where the answer is known. This also converts the "not novel" liability into the gauntlet asset.
- 3 novel-target systems from the Europa-like band after a mandatory per-system
  `literature_check.py` + corpus grep (Galilean pairs are the most-published territory in this
  field — `#577`'s 0/36 novelty-clear verdict is the cautionary precedent). On current knowledge:
  Jupiter-Ganymede, Uranus-Titania, Uranus-Ariel are the defensible first picks; one Saturnian
  µ~2-4e-6 moon (Rhea or Dione) as the deliberate band-edge probe.
- **Stage order inside the pilot**: family+eigenvalue survey across all ~40 coprime ratios × a
  coarse C-grid first (cheap, checkpointed via `#788`'s `campaign_runner`, `#800`'s thermal
  config `n_workers=4, pause_seconds_per_batch=30`); THEN connection attempts only on in-band
  cells. Stop-and-report checkpoint between the stages.
- Full-census dispatch is gated on: pilot finds ≥1 in-band novel-system connection AND the
  §2 writeback path is settled with `#781` written back through it.

## 4. Campaign #790 — periodic-chain itinerary enumeration

### 4.1 The one real data point contradicts the cost model

The proposal prices closures at "minutes-to-an-hour"; the project's only actual chain closure cost
five dispatches across ~8 days:

- `#768`: honest Newton stall (compounded instability ~1.2e14 over the ~4.2-period loop);
- `#773`: fail;
- `#775`: **decisive** fail — homotopy continuation accepted NOT ONE step across ~10 orders of
  magnitude of step size (290 map calls); the diagnosis was structural (no usable window between
  linearization-trust scale and the 1e-13 integration-noise floor), with a passing positive
  control, i.e. a property of the problem, not the harness;
- `#782`: success — but ONLY by recognizing the target as x-axis-symmetric and using the existing
  1-DOF C-pinned symmetric corrector (`correct_symmetric_fixed_jacobi`), which then converged in
  **3 Newton iterations**. The dispatch-assigned general technique (natural-waypoint multiple
  shooting) made progress but was invalidated by C-drift, because `#687`'s
  `correct_multiple_shooting` **does not constrain the Jacobi constant** — a real, named
  capability gap, still open;
- `#774`: the continuation of that one chain found a fold at ΔC≈7.0e-5 — 1.7% of the distance to
  the originally-targeted `C=3.01400` claim; verdict INCONCLUSIVE on the target claim.

So the honest per-closure cost bimodality is: **seconds, when the itinerary is symmetric and the
seed is good; effectively unbounded/intractable, when it is not** (with the current toolkit). An
enumeration over "hundreds-thousands of admissible itineraries" priced at the optimistic mode is
not a fundable estimate. `#774`'s fold is also a scope warning the proposal does not carry: even a
converged chain family can terminate at ΔC~1e-4 from its anchor, so "per system-energy" cells may
be far narrower in C than the atlas grid assumes.

### 4.2 The theoretical grounding is thinner than cited

`#749`'s Llibre-Martínez-Simó Theorem C is an existence proof of shift dynamics for the planar
**L2 Lyapunov family's** homoclinics at a countable mass-ratio sequence µ_k — a qualitative
existence licence, not a statement that periodic itineraries over *resonant-orbit* legs at *these*
systems' µ are numerically reachable. And the transversality that the shift construction rests on
was, in `#781`'s own corrected account, "asserted originally but never measured". Measuring
transversality of the atlas connections is cheap and should be a #790 admission gate on legs, not
an afterthought.

### 4.3 Recommended shape

**Defer the campaign; fund a symmetric-only mini-pilot as the unblocking probe.** Concretely:
enumerate short (length 2-3) x-axis-symmetric itineraries over whatever alphabet the #789 pilot
produces in its 3+1 systems, closed with the proven 1-DOF corrector — each attempt costs seconds
to minutes, so the whole probe is < a day of wall-clock. Its purpose is to measure the real
symmetric-closure hit rate on new systems (the number the full campaign's EV actually depends on).
The general (asymmetric) campaign stays blocked pending a **Jacobi-constrained multiple-shooting
corrector** (a well-defined `#687` extension — register it as its own task if #790 is to proceed;
this is the single highest-leverage build for the whole combinatorial program). The ML
seed-proposer question is correctly parked by the proposal itself ("benchmarked against the
zero-ML baseline") and needs no decision until a baseline exists.

Note in #790's favor: per §2, its converged chains are the one output class in this menu that is
genuinely catalogue-shaped (`#752`), and chain length/composition is genuinely unsearched. Deferral
here is sequencing, not skepticism about the target.

## 5. Campaign #791 — moon-tour encounter-sequence enumeration

### 5.1 The prior record and the wall

The entire prior record for this lane is `#501`: 6 sequences × 512 Sobol = 3072 cells, 213
feasible, **26 shot, 0 closed**, positive control (Liang Member D) PASSED. Two readings, both
supported: (a) the shooter works when the seed is near a real object, and the patched-conic
survivors simply weren't (a real negative); (b) 512 Sobol points in a 13-dimensional cell space
(`joint_sobol.py`: 1+3·n_legs dims; a length-8 sequence is 25 dims) is so sparse that (a) was
never really tested. The proposal's 4-32k cells/sequence does not change (b) materially at 25
dims. This is the quiet flaw in the scale-up: it multiplies **sequences** (lottery tickets) while
leaving per-sequence density at a level the one prior run already showed produces ~7% feasibility
and 0 closures. More sequences × same sparse depth = the same experiment, larger.

On the `#388` wall specifically: the proposal is right to flag it, and the risk is **real but
currently mismeasured**. The wall (shooters converge only to families whose basin contains the
seed; hit by three independent methods, 2026-06 notes) survived the `#830` energy-selectivity
re-check on 2026-08-12 (high-V∞ rows still don't recover anchors). BUT `#849` (registered the same
day) found `close_row_dsm`'s seeder reads `free_return_arcs` positionally — the `#820` defect
class — "so every negative it has produced on a descriptor-bearing row was computed under a posing
now known to be wrong for most of them" (`[[feedback_bugfix_invalidates_past_searches]]`). Running
#791's expensive n-body stage BEFORE `#849`'s reposing fix lands means interpreting new negatives
against a wall whose own calibration is known-contaminated. Cheap sequencing fix; do it in order.

### 5.2 Novelty ceiling

Even a closed Galilean tour faces the field's most saturated literature: `#577` found **0/36**
Galilean symmetric closures novelty-clear (Russell-Strange 2009 double-cyclers, Liang-Hernandez
triples, pump tours), and Saturnian tour design is Cassini/Titan-tour territory. The realistic
positive outcome of #791 is per-sequence empty-region stamps plus, at best, fresh instantiations
of known classes — a census deliverable, which `[[project_negative_results_registry]]` genuinely
values, but the proposal should be funded AS that, not as discovery.

### 5.3 Cost and mechanics

The ~1 s/cell patched-conic figure checks out against `#501`'s own record (3072 cells / 55 min ≈
1.07 s/cell), so Stage 1 at 10^6-10^7 cells is ~300-2800 CPU-hours — days on 8 cores, cheap
relative to the n-body stage. `joint_sobol.py` hardcodes `primary="Jupiter"` at `JointCell`
construction exactly as the proposal says; the parameterization is a small change (I did not audit
`joint_cell.py`'s evaluator for further Jupiter assumptions — do that before promising Saturn).
The 7 already-stamped sequences are correctly planned as SKIPPED via capability-subsumption.

### 5.4 Recommended shape

Restructure into gated stages, with the proposal's own cheap-first instinct made mandatory:

- **Stage 0 (hours)**: Tisserand/energy-graph pruning of the sequence alphabet itself — pure
  algebra, before any cell is evaluated. Report the surviving sequence count; if 10^3 sequences
  survive at length ≤ 8, the pruning is too weak to proceed.
- **Stage 1 (days)**: patched-conic enumeration of ALL surviving sequences via `campaign_runner`,
  per-sequence empty-region stamps, NO shooting. Mandatory stop-and-report: the per-sequence
  feasible-cell landscape is the decision input for whether an n-body stage is warranted at all,
  and for concentrating density (fewer sequences, more cells each) rather than spreading thin.
- **Stage 2 (only if funded separately, after `#849` lands)**: n-body shooting on the top decile,
  positive control (`#501`'s Liang Member D) re-run first.

The learned cell-ranking idea is correctly self-gated by the proposal ("deterministic gates first,
ML only if measured against them"); nothing to decide until Stage 1 produces the training signal.
