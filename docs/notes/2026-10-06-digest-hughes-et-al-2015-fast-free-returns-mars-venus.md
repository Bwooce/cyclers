## 0. Verdict

**No collision with `#942` ev-A or ev-C.**
- The Venus content is one-shot Earth-Venus-Earth (EVE) free returns, hosted by Earth, for an
  "Inspiration Venus" human flyby. The launch dates are 2019-2027, with Earth arrival v_inf at most
  9 km/s and a Venus flyby altitude of at least 300 km.
- Nothing repeats. Opportunities recur on the 1.6-yr synodic period and repeat in inertial space about
  every 8 yr (p.21).
- The paper's only Earth-Venus cycler statement credits Hollister 1969 and H&M 1970 (held).

## 1. Content (READ)

- **Mars free returns:**
  - searched 2015-2060 with STOUR (patched conic, analytical ephemeris);
  - fast opportunities are rare, repeating about every 15 yr, or every 32 yr with a Venus flyby;
  - candidates are on the EVME path, for example a 22 November 2021 launch with a 582-d flight time;
  - includes an entry analysis.
- **Venus (sec. E, pp.20-22, Figs. 23-24, Table 10):**
  - EVE candidates from 2019-2027, a Pareto set mostly in 2026, with lower-arrival-v_inf cases in 2019
    and 2021;
  - all have perihelion at least about 0.72 AU.
- **Cycler references (p.4):** Rall & Hollister 1971 "found to exist" Earth-Mars cyclers. The Earth-Venus
  cyclers are Hollister 1969 and H&M 1970.
