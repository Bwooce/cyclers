## 0. Verdict

**The historical source of the Copenhagen classification (classes a, b, c, ... n) for the restricted
problem with equal masses, as used by Hénon 1965a/b, Hénon 1973 and Bruno-Varin.**
- A review of the Copenhagen school's numerical periodic-orbit work since about 1913.
- Sections by class (headings read from the OCR, section numbers as printed):
  - I: librations around L_1 (class a);
  - II: retrograde orbits around L_1 (class b, the mirror of a);
  - IV and V: orbits around L_2 and L_3 (classes d, e);
  - Moulton's fixed-axis orbits around both masses (class l);
  - class m;
  - class k;
  - X and XI: retrograde orbits around m_2 and m_1 (classes f, h; "Tableau II" gives the development of
    f);
  - XII and XIII: direct orbits around m_2 and m_1 (classes g, i);
  - XIV: asymmetric re-entrant retrograde orbits (class n);
  - XV: a retrograde libration-like class.
- This is a history and taxonomy anchor only. For numbers, use Hénon 1965a/b and 1973. The figures are
  the Copenhagen characteristic and orbit drawings.
