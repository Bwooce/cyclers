## 0. Verdict

**One-shot manned multi-planet flyby ROUND TRIPS, not cyclers. No repeating trajectory. The R1(c) prior
art is the sequences themselves.**
- **"The relative geometry of Earth, Venus and Mars is approximately repetitive every 6.4 years; therefore
  opportunities for similar free flight mission profiles occur every 6.4 years"** (sec. I).
  - 6.4 yr = 4 Venus synodic periods, which is about 3 Mars synodic periods.
  - Compare VanderVeen 1969: the "absolute" Venus-Mars period is 32 yr = 5 x 6.4.
- Constraints: Earth departure v_inf <= 0.30 EMOS; Mars <= 0.30 EMOS; Venus <= 0.50 EMOS; total
  <= 750 d; periapsis >= 1.0 planet radius; powered flyby dV <= 3.0 km/s.
- **The missions found (1975-1980):**

| Year | Sequence | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 1975 | E-M-V-E, powered at Mars | Depart 485 km LEO (dV 5.92 km/s). Mars at +226 d, v_inf 0.1594 to 0.1726 EMOS, 0.558 km/s impulse, bend 44.3 deg, r_p 1.1 R_M. Venus free flyby 26 Sep 1976 (+232 d), r_p 1.25 R_V, v_inf .3972 EMOS. Earth 8 Feb 1977 (+135 d), reentry 13.0 km/s. Total 560-640 d |
| 1977 | E-V-M-V-E triple, slight power at Venus 1 | Depart 14 Feb 1977 (4.400 km/s). V +118 d, M +172 d, V again +259 d, E +127 d, arriving 22 Dec 1978. Total 674-679 d. Reentry 12.9-13.8 km/s |
| 1978 | E-V-M-E free return | Depart Dec 1978. V +160 d (r_p 1.17 R_V), M +240 d (r_p 1.14 R_M; near Mars aphelion), E +250 d, Sep 1980. Total 650 d. The Mars-Earth leg passes inside Earth's orbit |
| 1978 | E-V-M-E powered at Mars | About 175 d shorter than the free return. Reentry 16.74 km/s against 14.46 km/s |

- These are the Venus-swingby Mars round-trip lineage (Sohn 1964, Hollister & Prussing 1965,
  Deerwester 1965, Titus "Powered flybys of Mars"). The figures were not digitised; the numbers above are
  from the text.
- **R1(c) relevance:**
  - The 1977 E-V-M-V-E and 1978 E-V-M-E sequences are published prior art for any candidate whose single
    period contains the same encounter order. They are not periodic, so they are not a cycler collision.
  - The 6.4-yr near-repeat statement is a published basis for E-V-M phasing (4:3 synodic).
