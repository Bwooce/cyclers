## Cycler content
- The candidate list (pp. 13-24) carries option 10, "Saturn Orbiter; TITAN & ENC Cycler" / "Titan/Enc (cycler)",
  rated low science return (fewer Enceladus flybys than Titan flybys, not enough advance on Cassini).
- Page 3-7 (PDF p. 34, read on image): "Multiple Titan flybys, by a Saturn orbiter either in a dedicated orbit for Titan
  science, or an Enceladus-Titan cycler, were not judged to offer adequate advance relative to Cassini. ... the
  scientific return would be limited to a modest number of flybys (more modest for the cycler than a dedicated orbit)".
- No cycler numbers anywhere: no period, V_inf, legs, resonances or delta-V for a cycler. The cycler was dropped
  before the trajectory design stage.
- Non-cycler trajectory numbers, page 5-15 (PDF p. 62, read on image), for the chosen Saturn Orbiter with Enceladus
  flybys: Enceladus-resonant orbit, period 12.33 days, one Enceladus flyby per revolution, flyby V_inf "about 4.2 km/s",
  16 Enceladus encounters in about 200 days, solar phase varies less than 7 deg, then a Titan apsidal-rotation tour of
  ~180 deg, a second 200-day campaign, 32 Enceladus flybys in total. This is a single-moon resonant orbit, not a cycler.
