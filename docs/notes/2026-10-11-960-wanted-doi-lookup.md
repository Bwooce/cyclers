# #960 wanted-papers list: DOI / identifier lookup (2026-10-11, refs2-sonnet)

Scope: every row of `2026-10-05-960-wanted-papers.md` whose full text or preprint is still wanted. The wanted list was
not edited. Skipped rows (content held in another form, recovered or not available): 2, 3, 6, 7, 8, 15, 18, 65.
Rows 11 and 12 are kept because their journal forms are cheap to confirm.

## Method and status meanings

- CONFIRMED: the identifier was checked against a record. The record is a Crossref API response (`/works/<doi>`),
  an NTRS API record, a Keldysh library page, or an ADS scan (`articles.adsabs.harvard.edu/pdf/<bibcode>` returned a PDF;
  page 1 was viewed where the notes say so). Year, venue, volume and pages were compared with the row text.
- UNCONFIRMED: a plausible identifier was found, but only through a web-search summary or by inference, or the record
  is a related item that may not be the exact paper.
- NOT FOUND: the searches listed in the notes found no record.
- ADS abstract pages (`ui.adsabs.harvard.edu`) refuse scripted fetches (HTTP 405). The scan host `articles.adsabs.harvard.edu`
  works for the older volumes and is the open-access copy cited below.
- Crossref searches used `query.bibliographic`. NTRS searches used the public `ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/search` endpoint.
  The "NTRS record, no download" wording means the API listed no PDF download for that record.
