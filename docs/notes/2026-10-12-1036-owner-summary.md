## 1. What the candidates are

One massive flyby body (Charon, GM 106.1, floor 100 km) and one massless target moon (Styx, Nix, Kerberos or
Hydra), on circular coplanar orbits about the Pluto-Charon system. The cycle is [Charon block, Charon to moon,
moon pass-through, moon to Charon], one visit per cycle, period k synodic periods (k = 3 to 6, 23 to 56 d).
This is the Russell and Strange one-working-body architecture (AAS 07-118, JGCD 2009) at a system they did
not treat. Every candidate is an exact zero (residual under 1e-8 km/s), passes the #888/#937 demanded-turn gate
at the registry floors at every Charon flyby, and re-flies with DOP853 to under 1 km at every encounter.

## 4. Caveat on every strong member: deep dips

Every strong member flies inside the binary, where the barycentre monopole is not the field (#1024 sec. 2 and 8.5).
For the 171: r_min / a_Charon is 0.018 to 0.83 (median 0.25; 17 members below 0.1, 88 below 0.25); 5 to 50 percent
of the Lambert-leg time (median 15 percent; 26 members above 30 percent) is spent inside 2 a_Charon. The lowest
member is 306 km from the barycentre; it stays at least 1,826 km from Pluto's centre (638 km above its surface).
Eleven members pass within 1,500 km of Pluto's centre, 39 within 2,000 km; Pluto's atmosphere is not modelled.
The Pluto-surface screen that moved 48 labels in #1024 is evaluated in the same region, so those labels are no
more reliable than the monopole there. The patched-conic validity inside a few Charon radii is the main open
physical question of the whole set.

  not-found. Howett's topology label is empty on purpose (its four periodic orbits in the Pluto/Charon restricted
  problem are shown only as figures, with encounters of minor satellites below 300 m/s); an unlabelled anchor can only
  cap a signature at inconclusive. The comparison with Howett's Figs. 13 and 14 is a human step.
- At systems the Russell-Strange anchors do not cover the gate returns "known-architecture-new-system" (control C2,
  the same signature with primary Jupiter), which is the reading #875 (ii) uses for a known architecture at a system
  never treated.
- The other Pluto anchors do not match: Stern et al. 2020 (mga-tour, one working body), Showalter and Hamilton
  2015 and Brozovic et al. (ephemeris), the 2025 tadpole preprint (binary-coorbital).
