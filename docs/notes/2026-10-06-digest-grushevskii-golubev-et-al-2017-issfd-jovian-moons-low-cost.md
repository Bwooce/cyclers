## 0. Verdict (sent to twobody-gen2-opus)

**No collision with `#943` gc-1, gc-2 or ge-1..3.**
- It is the same "cross"-GAM template as Golubev 2014 (held): a G...G solo chain, a cross reflection to
  Callisto, a C...C solo chain, and a re-reflection back to Ganymede.
  - Formula (1) is G1..Gk, Gm..Gmn, Gk+1..: a one-way v_inf-reduction and radiation-dose (TID) device for
    a Ganymede lander.
  - It is worked on "Multi-Tisserand" (Ti-P) graphs in a two-coupled-CR3BP (G/C) model and a full
    ephemeris, with millions of trajectory-beam variants (about 3e6 primary, about 3e3 after cross-GAM
    selection).
- **No periodic G-C sequence and no per-encounter v_inf.** The only numbers are the limits used:
  - spacecraft periods of 12 and 18 Earth days, and 70 d;
  - TID limits of 21 and 11 krad per revolution;
  - "comfortable" tours under 70 krad, against the JUICE norm of 300 krad;
  - v_inf reduction "within 0.5 km/s";
  - Ganymede period 7.155 d.
- The resonance lines on the 3-D surface (3:4, 1:1, 5:4, 4:3, 3:2, 5:3) are Tisserand-graph
  bookkeeping, not cycles.
- The two-working-body G-C cycler record remains Campagnola 2019 GCGC alone.
