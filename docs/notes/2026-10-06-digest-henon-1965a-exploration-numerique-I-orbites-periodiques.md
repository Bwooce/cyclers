## 0. Verdict

**Paper I of the Hénon mu = 1/2 series: the map of all simple-periodic symmetric orbits for equal masses.**
- The method: successive crossings of the x-axis, giving characteristic curves in the (x, C) plane
  (Figs. 3-9).
- There are 21 classes, 6 of them new (o, r, s, t, w, x, y, z in the tables, plus the limiting branch
  c_inf). Strömgren 1935 and Bartlett 1964/65 are merged in.
- **Family h: no table here.** It appears only in the characteristic figures (Fig. 3). The paper refers
  to Bartlett's tables for the classes it does not tabulate.
- So, for the Bruno-Varin mu = 0.5 gap, the numbers come from Hénon 1965b (four digits) and Hénon 1973
  (six digits), not from this paper.
- What it adds on h (p.8):
  - h is the mirror of f (retrograde satellites of the other primary).
  - The ejection orbit splits h into h and h_2, which starts at x = +1/2.
  - An n-periodic orbit is shown by 1 point when n is odd and 0 or 2 points when n is even.
- Tabulated: classes o, r, s, t, w, x, y and z, and c_inf (C, x crossings, T/2).
  - All the limiting periodic-asymptotic orbits have C = 11/4.
  - The constants of eq. (9) were recomputed and agree with the print.
- **Use:** classification background for `#944`, `#956` and the `#948` second-species work at
  mu = 1/2. This is the source of the family letters used by Hénon 1965b, Hénon 1973 and Bruno-Varin.
