## 0. Verdict

A CR3BP resonant-orbit tutorial thesis (Howell group), with three uses:
- **Planar resonant families** (Ch. 3.3, "Families of Planar Resonant Orbits", with family ends,
  stability and bifurcations; 3-D resonant orbits in 3.6). The family-ID background for `#957` R10 and
  `#947` R3, including the 2:1 and 3:1 families (Table 4.6 gives 3:1 and 2:1 initial conditions).
- **Transfers on resonant arcs and manifolds** (Ch. 4):
  - Earth-Moon, Saturn-Titan, and Mars-Deimos (a 3:2 then 2:1 resonant tour, Fig. 4.5);
  - a 5:6 resonant orbit to an L2 Lyapunov orbit (Saturn-Titan).
- **Homoclinic and heteroclinic connections and "resonant orbit chains"** (Ch. 4.6-4.7):
  - 3:5 and 3:4 resonant homoclinics in Saturn-Titan at C = 3.0013 (Tables 4.10-4.12 give initial
    conditions and C);
  - **periodic orbits that cycle between two resonance geometries.**
  - This is single-moon (CR3BP) resonance switching, a relative of the Anderson-Lo resonant-flyby
    chains. It is not a two-moon cycler. Check against `#947` / `#957` candidates only if a candidate is
    a single-secondary resonance-switching periodic orbit.
- No collision with the two-working-body candidates (gc/ge, ev) is possible: there is one secondary per
  model.
